# Anti-vibe design review

This skill helps catch generic AI-web style patterns that reduce trust or polish.

## Warning signs

- over-stacked trend design patterns,
- decorative visuals without clear hierarchy,
- weak contrast and low information clarity,
- generic blocks that hide important content,
- motion or effects that distract from actual functionality.

## Review principle

Use visual clarity, information hierarchy, and product purpose before trend imitation.
