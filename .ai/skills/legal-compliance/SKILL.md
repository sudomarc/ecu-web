# Legal and compliance review

This skill applies when user flows involve data collection, cookies, consent, account flows, uploads, subscriptions, marketing, or privacy-sensitive features.

## Audit areas

- data collection and purpose transparency,
- storage and cookie consent,
- account and authentication flows,
- uploads and content handling,
- subscriptions and billing flows,
- retention, deletion, and privacy controls.

## Rule

Do not assume compliance. Verify the actual project behavior and the applicable legal requirements for the region and business context.
